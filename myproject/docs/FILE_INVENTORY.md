## Status Vocabulary

- `CURRENT`: current paper-oriented entry point or required current data.
- `SUPPORTING`: directly supports the current route, or documents its data/model contract.
- `LEGACY_BASELINE`: retained historical or comparison implementation; not the current entry point.
- `EXPERIMENTAL`: variant, helper, backup, or item whose role is not fully confirmed.
- `GENERATED`: reproducible output, cache, log, model, image, or pointer.
- `SMOKE_TEST`: generated by a short connectivity run; metrics are not scientific evidence.
- `STALE_DOCUMENTATION`: retained historical documentation whose mainline or schema statements are outdated.
- `NEEDS_REVIEW`: do not delete or reinterpret without checking its producer and consumers.

## Maintenance Rules

- Do not delete or move a file solely because it is marked legacy, experimental, or needs review.
- When a new training route becomes authoritative, update this inventory and `TRAINING_ENGINEERING.md` together.
- When a `.mat` export changes shape or field meaning, update [PINN_DATA_README.md](PINN_DATA_README.md) and record the change in [RESEARCH_IDEA.md](RESEARCH_IDEA.md).
- Treat actual code, HDF5 structure, `run_config.json`, and `metrics.json` as higher-priority evidence than older README prose.
